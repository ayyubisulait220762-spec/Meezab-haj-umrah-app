Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Meezab E Rahemat app (“the app”, “we”, “us”) collects, why, and what choices you have. The app is run by Meezab E Rahemat Haj, Umrah and Ziyarat Tours, 461/B, Khayaban Nishat Chowk, Motipura, Malegaon, Maharashtra, India.

In short: we only collect what you choose to send us (your registration details or feedback), we use it only to help you with Hajj and Umrah, we never sell it, and the app has no advertising and no tracking.

1. Information you give us

WhenWhatWhy
Register interestYour name, mobile number, city, the package you are interested in, number of travellers, best time to call, app language.So our team can call you and guide you about Hajj and Umrah packages.
FeedbackYour star rating and the comment you write.To improve our service. With your permission we may share kind words (without your mobile number) as a testimonial.

This information is sent to our private Google Sheet, which only our team can open. If you also press Send in WhatsApp when the app opens it, the message is sent through WhatsApp under WhatsApp’s own privacy policy.

If you have no internet when you submit, the request is kept on your phone and sent automatically when you are back online.

2. Information that stays on your phone

  • Location (only if you tap “Use my location”). Used on your phone to show prayer times for your city and the Qibla direction. It is not sent to us. You can choose your city from the list instead.
  • Compass / motion sensor (only if you tap “Start compass”). Used on your phone to turn the Qibla compass. Not sent to us.
  • Hajj 2027 share card. The name and city you type are used to draw the card on your phone. The card is shared only if you choose to share it.
  • Settings. Your language, sound on/off, the selected prayer-times city and similar choices are saved on your phone so the app remembers them.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your information.
  • We do not show advertisements.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not collect payment details in the app. Documents and payments are handled at our office.

4. Who can see your information

Only the Meezab E Rahemat team (our founder, managing director and tour team) uses your details to contact and assist you. Your registration is stored using Google’s services (Google Sheets), which keep it on our behalf. When you book a package, some details are shared with the authorities and service providers needed for your journey (for example for visa, flights and hotels), as required by law and the Hajj regulations.

5. Links to other apps

The app has buttons that open WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Google Maps and phone calls. These open the other app or website, which has its own privacy policy.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your registration details while we are assisting you and for up to 3 years after your last contact with us, so we can follow up about future Hajj and Umrah journeys, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your details, or to stop contacting you. Contact us using the details below and we will act within 30 days. Information that stays only on your phone can be removed by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app.

8. Security

Information is sent over a secure (HTTPS) connection. The app checks each entry and does not contain any passwords or keys. Access to our Sheet is limited to our team.

9. Children

The app is meant for adults planning Hajj and Umrah. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If a child has sent us details, please contact us and we will delete them.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
